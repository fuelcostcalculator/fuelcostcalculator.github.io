Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Multiply distance by consumption, then by the fuel price. The calculator does it for you.

What is litres per 100 km?

It is how many litres your car uses to travel 100 km. Lower is better, because it means less fuel for the same distance.

How do I find my real consumption?

Fill the tank, note the distance, drive, then fill again and divide the litres used by the distance in hundreds of km.

Does driving style affect fuel use?

Yes. Smooth, steady driving usually uses less fuel than hard acceleration and braking.

Is an EV cheaper to run than a petrol car?

On energy alone it often is, but it depends on your local fuel and electricity prices. Enter your own prices in the comparison tool.

How do I budget fuel for a month?

Multiply your daily distance by your driving days, then work out the fuel and the cost from your consumption and price.

What is the difference between mpg and litres per 100 km?

Both measure consumption. MPG is miles per gallon, higher is better. Litres per 100 km is litres used per 100 km, lower is better.

Why is my real consumption higher than the brochure?

Brochure figures are measured in test conditions. Real traffic, load, weather and driving style usually raise consumption.
