Fuel Cost per Kilometre Calculator

Find out what each kilometre or mile costs you in fuel.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Enter your own fuel price; prices vary by place and day.

How this calculator works

Cost per kilometre is consumption per 100 km divided by 100, multiplied by the price per litre.

Worked example

At 8 litres per 100 km and 2.50 per litre, fuel costs 0.20 per km, or 20 per 100 km.
