Petrol vs EV Running Cost Comparison

Compare the monthly running cost of a petrol car with an electric car, using your own prices and clearly labelled assumptions.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Assumptions are labelled: prices and consumption are examples you should replace with your own. Energy cost only; purchase, servicing and insurance are not included.

How this calculator works

Petrol cost is monthly distance multiplied by litres per km and the petrol price. EV cost is monthly distance multiplied by kWh per km and the electricity price. This compares energy costs only, not purchase price, servicing or insurance.

Worked example

Driving 1,000 km a month: a petrol car at 8 litres per 100 km and 2.50 per litre costs 200. An EV at 16 kWh per 100 km and 0.40 per kWh costs 64. The difference is 136 a month, on these example prices.
